# Bunraku & Kabuki Bilingual Theater Companion (文楽・歌舞伎 同伴リーダー)

## Features

- **Expandable Scene Guide & Cast Card (`📖 Scene Guide & Cast`)**:
  - Sits directly above the bilingual script columns and collapses to a single compact bar during live theater following.
  - Displays the active scene's **Play Category** (*Sewamono* / *Jidaimono* / *Michiyuki*), **Playwright**, and **Historical Premiere**.
  - Expands to show a detailed **Scene Synopsis**, **Stage & Musical Highlights ("What to Watch For")**, and **Key Characters in This Scene** (with Kanji, English names, hidden identities, and Bunraku *Kashira* puppet head or Kabuki *Yakugara* role classifications).
- **Info, Tickets & Reference Modal Hub (`🎟️ Info & Tickets`)**:
  - **🎟️ Tickets & Venue Tab**: Direct links to official Japanese and English box offices (National Theatre Ticket Centre, Ticket Web Shochiku, Kabukiweb, Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre Box Office), full seating tier pricing, Single-Act (*Hitomakumi-seki* 一幕見席) instructions, station exit access & Google Maps links, and lobby amenities (English Earphone Guide rates, *Yukahon* / *Sujigaki* program booklets, and free English flyers).
  - **📚 Reference & Drive Tab**: One-click cards for the mounted **Project Google Drive Study Materials** (Project Folder, Detailed Play Guide Google Doc, Bilingual Master Script Sheet, and 3 Scanned Playbill PDFs), **Official Digital Portals** (JAC *Bunka Digital Library*, UNESCO *Invitation to Bunraku*, *Kabuki-bito*, *Kabuki21*), **English Translations & Scholarship** (Donald Keene, C. Andrew Gerstle, Stanleigh H. Jones Jr., James R. Brandon & Samuel L. Leiter), and **Classical Japanese Librettos** (Iwanami *Shin Nihon Koten Bungaku Ta系*, Shinchōsha, Official *Yukahon*).
  - **📜 Program Study Guide Tab**: Complete offline-readable program commentary for all parts of the selected performance (including the full 3-part *Kinshu Bunraku Kōen* guide covering *Shinjū Yoigōshin*, *Imoseyama Onna Teikin*, and *Honchō Nijūshikō*).
  - **🎭 Theater Glossary Tab**: Searchable bilingual glossary of essential Bunraku and Kabuki terminology (*Tayū*, *Futozao Shamisen*, *Sannin-zukai*, *Kashira*, *Yukahon*, *Ningyō-buri*, *Mie*, *Hanamichi*, *Mawari-butai*, *Geza*, *Yakugara*, *Sewamono*, *Jidaimono*, *Giri-Ninjō*, *Michiyuki*, *Modori*, *Hitomakumi-seki*).
- **Performance & Date Switcher**: Two-level header selector to switch between saved Bunraku and Kabuki programs and their scenes, with per-performance progress saved in `localStorage`.
- **AMOLED Theater Dark Mode**: Pure black/charcoal palette with low glare for theater etiquette, plus a Day Mode toggle (`☀️` / `🌙`).
- **Synchronized Dual-Column View**: Japanese chanting & dialogue with toggleable `<ruby>` furigana on the left; English translation and cultural context notes on the right.
- **English Audio Narration (Voice Companion)**:
  - Hands-free continuous auto-advance with synchronized scrolling and line highlighting via the in-browser Web Speech API.
  - Per-line tap-to-read button (`🔈 Read`) and adjustable reading tempo (`1.0x`, `1.2x`, `0.8x`).
- **Live Follower Controls**: Line highlighting, auto-centering, keyboard shortcuts (`Space` / `↓` next, `Shift+Space` / `↑` prev, `P` or `K` play/pause audio, `Esc` close modal), and floating one-handed touch controls.

## Adding Future Performances (Bunraku or Kabuki)
1. Create `data/<performance-id>.js` assigning `window.PERFORMANCE_DATA["<performance-id>"]` to an array of line objects (`Scene_ID`, `Scene_Title`, `Line_No`, `Speaker`, `Japanese_Ruby`, `Japanese_Plain`, `English`, `Notes`).
2. Register the performance metadata (`id`, `label`, `subtitle`, `genre: "bunraku" | "kabuki"`, `dataFile`, `venue`, `ticketing`, `resources`, `studyGuide`, and `parts`) in `data/performances.js`.
